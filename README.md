"Nike Sales Performance Dashboard"

Project Overview:

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze and uncover insights from Nike global sales data. This project transforms raw commercial data into a comprehensive visual story, tracking performance across various financial, demographic, and product dimensions to drive informed business decisions.

Key Features and Insights:

1. Financial Performance: Tracks overall profitability, highlighting an impressive 3M in total revenue and 964K in total profit.

2. Seasonality and Trends: A dedicated line analysis reveals clear monthly sales fluctuations, identifying peak consumer demand surges during May and November.

3. Sales Channel Analysis: Compares the revenue contributions of different distribution models, balancing Retail, Online, and Distributor channels.

4. Customer Segment Insights: Breaks down profitability across key consumer groups, including Youth, Casual, Athletes, and Premium categories.

5. Demographics: Evaluates purchase volume (Units Sold) across different age groups and genders to identify core target markets.

6. Product Performance: Uses Treemap visualizations to quickly isolate high-performing categories across Apparel, Shoes, and Accessories.

7. Geographic Distribution: Integrates an interactive map view showcasing country-level sales contributions and market hotspots.

Tools and Skills Demonstrated:

Data Preparation and Cleaning: Managed within Microsoft Excel to preprocess, clean, and structure the raw dataset for accurate analysis.

Data Visualization and Storytelling: Developed a cohesive user interface utilizing interactive slicers, custom color palettes aligned with the brand, and intuitive chart selections to maximize insights at a glance.

Project Objective:

The goal of this dashboard is to provide stakeholders with actionable business intelligence. By identifying high-performing product categories, understanding customer demographics, and highlighting peak sales seasons, this tool helps optimize inventory placement, marketing spend, and channel strategies.

How to View the Project:

1. Download the .pbix file from this repository.
2. Open it using Power BI Desktop.
3. Interact with the slicers (Age Group, Gender, Channel) to explore the data dynamically.
